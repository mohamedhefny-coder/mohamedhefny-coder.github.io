This Privacy Policy describes how Amazing Anatomy handles information when you use the website and the mobile app.

Information we may collect

  • App usage data: basic interaction and performance signals to improve the product.
  • Device information: such as device model, OS version, and language (where provided by the platform).
  • Notifications: if you enable push notifications, the platform may provide a token to deliver messages.

Advertising

We may display ads on supported platforms. On the web, ads are shown only on content screens (not on error pages). Advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies depending on your browser and settings.

Third-party services

The project may use third-party services (for example: analytics, notifications, database services) to provide functionality. These services may process data according to their own policies.

Data retention

We keep data only as long as needed for the purposes described above, and we work to minimize the amount of information collected.

Contact

If you have questions, open an issue on GitHub: Support issues.
