Amazing Anatomy is an educational project designed to help learners explore human anatomy through interactive content, diagrams, and quizzes.

Educational purpose

The content is intended for study and revision (e.g., medical students, nursing students, and healthcare trainees). It is not medical advice.

Medical disclaimer

This website and app do not provide medical diagnosis or treatment guidance. If you have medical concerns, consult a qualified healthcare professional.

Contact / support

For questions or issues, please use the GitHub repository: Amazing Anatomy on GitHub.
